My Top 5 Movies

These are five movies I enjoy because of their memorable stories, characters and messages.

  1. Black Panther (2018) — I enjoy its action, creativity and themes of leadership, identity and responsibility.
  2. Fast & Furious 7 (2015) — An entertaining action movie with exciting cars, teamwork and loyalty.
  3. Avengers: Endgame (2019) — A memorable superhero film about sacrifice, courage and working together.
  4. Interstellar (2014) — A thought-provoking science-fiction story about space, time, exploration and family.
  5. The Pursuit of Happyness (2006) — An inspiring story about determination, patience and overcoming challenges.
